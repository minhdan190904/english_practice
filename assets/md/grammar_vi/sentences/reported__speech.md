# 🗣️ Câu Tường Thuật (Reported Speech)

> Câu tường thuật (còn gọi là lời nói gián tiếp) dùng để thuật lại lời nói của người khác mà **không trích dẫn nguyên văn**. Khi chuyển sang câu tường thuật, thì động từ thường **lùi về quá khứ** (backshift).

---

## 🔄 Backshift — Lùi Thì Động Từ

| Lời nói trực tiếp | Lời nói gián tiếp |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Simple Present | Simple Past |
| Simple Past | Past Perfect |
| Present Continuous | Past Continuous |
| Present Perfect | Past Perfect |
| will | would |
| can | could |
| may | might |
| must | had to |
| shall | should |

✅ **Ví dụ:**
- Direct: *"I **live** in Hanoi."*
- Reported: She said she **lived** in Hanoi.

- Direct: *"I **have finished** the work."*
- Reported: He said he **had finished** the work.

---

## 👤 Thay Đổi Đại Từ (Pronouns)

> Đại từ phải thay đổi cho phù hợp với ngữ cảnh của người tường thuật.

| Trực tiếp | Gián tiếp (thường) |
|-----------|-------------------|
| I | he / she |
| my | his / her |
| me | him / her |
| we | they |
| our | their |
| you | I / we / they (tùy ngữ cảnh) |

✅ **Ví dụ:**
- Direct: *"**I** love **my** job."*
- Reported: He said **he** loved **his** job.

---

## 🕐 Thay Đổi Trạng Từ Thời Gian (Time Expressions)

| Trực tiếp | Gián tiếp |
|-----------|-----------|
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| last week | the week before |
| next week | the following week |
| here | there |
| this | that |
| these | those |
| ago | before |

---

## 📝 Các Loại Câu Tường Thuật

### 🔹 Tường thuật câu trần thuật — dùng **say / tell**

```
S + said (that) + clause
S + told + object + (that) + clause
```

- *"I'm tired."* → She **said** (that) she was tired.
- *"I'm tired."* → She **told me** (that) she was tired.

⚠️ **tell** luôn cần tân ngữ, **say** thì không:
- ✅ She told **me** she was tired.
- ❌ She said **me** she was tired.

---

### 🔹 Tường thuật câu hỏi — dùng **ask / wonder**

**Câu hỏi Yes/No:**
```
S + asked + (object) + if/whether + clause
```
- *"Are you hungry?"* → He asked me **if** I was hungry.

**Câu hỏi có từ để hỏi:**
```
S + asked + (object) + wh-word + clause (dạng khẳng định)
```
- *"Where do you live?"* → She asked me **where** I lived.
- *"What time is it?"* → He asked **what time it was**.

---

### 🔹 Tường thuật câu mệnh lệnh — dùng **tell / ask / order**

```
S + told/asked/ordered + object + (not) + to-infinitive
```

- *"Close the door!"* → He told me **to close** the door.
- *"Don't be late!"* → She told me **not to be** late.
- *"Please help me."* → She asked me **to help** her.

---

## 💡 Lưu Ý: Không Cần Backshift Khi...

- Sự thật hiển nhiên, quy luật tự nhiên:
  - *"The sun rises in the east."* → He said the sun **rises** in the east. ✅
- Lời nói vừa mới nói xong (thì hiện tại vẫn còn đúng):
  - *"I am from Vietnam."* → She says she **is** from Vietnam. ✅

---

## 📊 Bảng Reporting Verbs Phổ Biến

| Động từ | Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|---------|----------|-------|
| **say** | say (that) | He said he was busy. |
| **tell** | tell sb (that) | She told me she would come. |
| **ask** | ask sb if/to-inf | He asked me if I was ready. |
| **promise** | promise to-inf | She promised to help. |
| **warn** | warn sb not to | He warned me not to go. |
| **suggest** | suggest + V-ing | She suggested going by bus. |
| **admit** | admit + V-ing | He admitted taking the money. |
| **deny** | deny + V-ing | She denied stealing it. |
